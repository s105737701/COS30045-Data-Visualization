Storyboards

1-"How does screen size impact energy consumption?"

Issue

People buy giant screens and get a shock when their electric bill arrives.

Demonstrate

Show a line graph with power usage spiking violently as screen size goes from 55 to 85 inches.

Ideas

Show the real-world dollar cost to run it, not just a star rating. And that's why it matters.

Describe

Print custom tags showing the "5-Year Electricity Cost" and stick them on TVs larger than 75 inches.

Show Before and After Survey

Show survey data where buyers opted for slightly smaller, more efficient TVs after seeing the dollar amount.

Recommendations

Truth in pricing works. Let's make 5-year running cost tags standard on all massive screens.

2-"How does screen type affect energy consumption?"

Issue

Buyers want amazing picture quality but don't realize it eats more power.

Demonstrate

Show a basic chart comparing power draw: basic LED is low, while OLED is high. Perfect black levels take more energy.

Ideas

Educate buyers on the tradeoff between picture tech and power bills.

Describe

Put an information card next to OLED TVs explaining why they use more power. Every single pixel makes its own light.

Show Before and After Survey

Show a customer survey taken three months later. Buyers weren't mad about their power bills because they knew what to expect.

Recommendations

Use a chart to show the difference in power draw between different screen types.
